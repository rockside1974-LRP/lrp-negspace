what this is
An approximation, not a placement. The book map has no true georeference. The map icons and the map image are carried through a best-fit alignment of the dashed path onto the Little Rock Pond loop (rotate ~206°, scale ~0.21). It is close, not exact. Nothing here is a solve.
Blue = where people have actually walked. About 1,700 public hiking recordings on the two LRP trails, aggregated. Faint blue is everyone on the trail; bright blue is where recordings wandered more than ~25 m off any trail. No names, no individual tracks.
Red = where people said they searched. Circles are places named in public posts; the dashed red band along the loop is the many "searched LRP, found nothing" reports that gave no spot.
The undocumented grade. The purple dashed lines are the state's trail layer (old logging grades used as sled routes). One of them leaves the AT at the tenting area and runs EAST across the north shoulder of Homer Stone Mountain. It is not on OpenStreetMap and not on the trailhead signboard. Hiker recordings follow it for only the first ~500 m; beyond that, and along the whole shoulder it crosses, is the one part of this map where very few people appear to have looked.
Layers: hillshade / ortho / canopy from public LIDAR and imagery · trails and pond · the 1727 ft contour · "250 ft band" = 50–250 ft from any mapped trail. Tap a red circle for its label.
