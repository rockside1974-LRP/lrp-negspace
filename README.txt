LITTLE ROCK POND — WHERE PEOPLE HAVE BEEN (v2026-09-03)
Open index.html in any browser (works offline). Drag/pinch to move; tap a red circle for its label.

This is an approximation, not a placement map. The book map has no real georeference; its icons and image are carried through a best-fit alignment of the dashed path onto the pond loop (rotate ~206°, scale ~0.21). Close, not exact. Not a solve.

BLUE = where people have actually walked: about 1,700 public hiking recordings on the two LRP trails, aggregated (faint = on trail, bright = wandered >25 m off trail). No names or individual tracks.
RED  = where people said they searched, from public posts (circles = a named spot; dashed band = "searched LRP, no spot given").
PURPLE dashed = the state's trail layer: old logging grades / sled routes not on OpenStreetMap or the signboard. The one leaving the AT at the tenting area and running EAST across Homer Stone's north shoulder is followed by hikers for only ~500 m. That shoulder is the one part of the map where very few people appear to have looked.
Other layers: hillshade / ortho / canopy, trails and pond, the 1727 ft contour, map icons and map overlay (with opacity slider), 250 ft trail band.
